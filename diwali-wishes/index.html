Light Belongs to Every Soul

While each of our traditions carries its own rich history and distinct colors, the essence of light knows no boundaries. A flame does not inquire who kindles it—it generously dispels darkness, radiates warmth, and illuminates the path ahead for everyone alike.

I celebrate this festival with deep respect for its universal virtues: humanity comes first, kindness transcends differences, and the hope for renewed beginnings, peace, and prosperity belongs to all of us.

May this season inspire genuine fellowship across our teams, communities, and families. Here is wishing you, your colleagues, and your loved ones limitless joy, good health, and flourishing success.

With heartfelt warmth & respect, Osama Khalil
